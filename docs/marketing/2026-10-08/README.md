## Recommendation for discussion

Use free, useful map discovery to build Pini's supply and buyer attention. Let Aura earn trust as the separate agency. Test one narrow Premium service only after learning what customers will pay for; avoid launching three unproven offers together.

The competitor claim needs correction: good existing property products do exist. Pini's opportunity is to make its supported categories easier to understand, explore and contact with current information—not to claim it is the first map or that nobody else offers a useful product.

## Boundaries

Pini stays map-first and includes new-building projects, houses and land. Individual flat listings are excluded; apartment selection inside a new-building project's explorer remains valid. Project listings are free. Free house/land pricing and the exact Premium service are not yet decided.

Aura stays an independent Georgian residential-complex agency serving Georgian, Russian, Hebrew and English audiences. Its approved longer cinematic homepage is retained. Marketing cooperation is proposed; private customer transfer and automatic referral are not implied.
