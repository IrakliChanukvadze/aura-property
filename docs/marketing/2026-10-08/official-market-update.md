# Official evidence update: 2026

Reviewed 8 October 2026. The orchestrator independently read these three Geostat releases after specialist research. This supersedes the earlier 2025 digital baseline for current planning; retain historical figures as dated evidence.

## Digital access

June 2026 fieldwork, published 2 September: household internet access is 93.5% nationally, 98.2% in Tbilisi and 96.3% in Adjara. Social-network participation is 97.5% among internet users aged 15+ who used the internet in the preceding three months. Wireless access using a mobile device is 99.8% for that user population; the definition includes laptops and tablets. Sample size was not stated in the release inspected. These figures are not smartphone website traffic, platform ad reach or property-buying intent. [Geostat ICT 2026](https://www.geostat.ge/media/82392/Indicators-of-Using-Information-and-Communication-Technologies-%28ICT%29-in-Households.pdf).

Planning inference: digital discovery is plausible in both regions. Test social distribution and actual mobile buyer tasks, but select platforms through observed results. The access difference alone does not justify excluding Adjara or forecast Pini's audience.

## Construction geography

January–June 2026, published 30 July: 5,391 construction permissions; Tbilisi accounts for 49.2% of the count and Adjara for 7.6%. Coverage includes reconstruction and new construction across residential, commercial, hotel, industrial, agricultural and other buildings. These are not counts of available residential projects or houses. [Geostat permits release](https://geostat.ge/media/81764/Information-about-Permissions-Granted-for-Construction-and-Completed-Objects.pdf).

Planning inference: Tbilisi is a defensible first supplier-research geography, conditional on actual authorized residential inventory. Do not convert permits into onboardable projects, leads or sales. Keep Adjara as a distinct expansion candidate; building types and scales differ.

## Price context

Q2 2026, published 23 July: the Residential Property Price Index increased 0.9% quarterly and 4.9% annually. Despite the national-sounding title, coverage is new dwellings in Tbilisi: flats and detached houses, in GEL. Inputs are scraped offer prices with hedonic adjustment, not completed sale prices. No land or Batumi coverage. [Geostat RPPI Q2 2026](https://www.geostat.ge/media/81560/Residential-Property-Price-Index---II-quarter-of-2026.pdf).

Planning inference: dated price education can explain currency, frame/condition and comparable properties. This is neither Boulevard's price nor an investment-return promise. Index coverage does not change Pini's exclusion of standalone flat listings or Aura's residential-complex scope.

## Decision impact

Recommend a focused Tbilisi learning pilot if supplier completeness and response capacity support it. Keep the national map and all three Pini categories. Reserve Adjara expansion for its own inventory and buyer evidence. Channel costs, conversion, virality, creator fees and agency closing rates remain unverified; these releases cannot supply them. The [pilot protocol](pilot-decision-protocol.md) defines how to collect the missing business evidence.
