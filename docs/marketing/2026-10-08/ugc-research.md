# Pini / Aura UGC research and production decision

Prepared 8 October 2026 (Asia/Tbilisi). Research only: no media generation, purchase, outreach, upload, account change or publishing. Prices checked against official vendor pages on this date. Dollar amounts exclude tax and currency conversion. Website/marketing recommendations are proposals, not confirmed business requirements.

## Decision

Use real captured websites and approved architectural media as the reliable core. Make natural Georgian speech a separately approved asset. For the existing Boulevard film, preserve the original 40-second version the owner liked and test **video + approved audio → targeted lip correction** on one 5–8-second speaking shot. Do not regenerate a talking person from one still photo as the default repair method: that path already failed the owner's identity, movement and appearance expectations. No paid test is authorized by this research deliverable.

For ongoing brand content, a half-day recording session with the actual consenting actor is the strongest production hypothesis. Film a library of hooks, gestures, presenter passages and clean background shots; reuse approved renders, real site capture and exact audio. AI can help with supplementary camera movement and language adaptation after small tests pass. It must not invent construction progress, apartment views or amenities.

## What the evidence does and does not establish

* Meta reports 34.5% lower cost per result for vertical Reels video with sound and safe-zone messages versus image ads, based on 15 split tests across e-commerce, retail and consumer goods. This supports a vertical format experiment; it is **not** a Georgia/property lead-cost forecast. [Meta Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads).
* TikTok recommends a clear early hook, full-screen vertical framing and usually 21–34-second ads. Its current guide also recommends refreshing creative every 2–4 weeks. These are platform guidelines, not a guarantee that 30 seconds beats the owner's preferred 40-second brand film. [TikTok video guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/video-advertising-guide).
* In NAR's 2025 US member survey, 39% of respondents chose social media as a technology producing the highest number of quality leads; CRM was 23%. This is a **share of respondents**, not “39% of all leads came from social media.” The survey is useful directional evidence for combining content and a lead follow-up system, not Georgian market demand. [NAR survey, page 9](https://cms.nar.realtor/sites/default/files/2025-09/2025-realtors-technology-survey-report-09-18-2025.pdf).
* TikTok paid-market availability depends on account registration and targeting market. Georgia is absent from its published self-service account creation list. Treat TikTok as an organic test channel first; verify legitimate account/market availability before budgeting paid Georgian delivery. [TikTok creation markets](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/available-countries-and-regions-for-ad-account-creation-in-bc?lang=ru), [targeting availability](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/placements-available-locations?lang=en).

## Voice and lip-sync findings

ElevenLabs' current documentation lists Georgian in v3 and v4, alongside Russian and English. Multilingual v2, Flash v2.5 and the documented speech-to-speech v2 list do not include Georgian. A generic “multilingual” setting therefore does not prove Georgian compatibility. Higgsfield's exposed ElevenLabs variant does not identify its underlying model; the owner's approved Grady take is the better empirical reference. Freeze it, except owner-approved word patches. [ElevenLabs models](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models).

Higgsfield's Audio launch article lists English/Russian translation but omits Georgian. Its broad marketing claims of multilingual video should not be interpreted as verified Georgian dubbing. [Higgsfield Audio](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-audio-ai-voice-tools).

Sync's official sync-3 documentation accepts video plus supplied audio and describes preserving speaking cadence/expression. It reports 95+ languages, but the page does not enumerate Georgian; Georgian success remains a test requirement. Unlike image animation, video input can start from the accepted actor performance. Vendor realism claims are not independent validation. [sync-3 documentation, updated 2 October 2026](https://sync.so/docs/models/sync-3).

Proposed audition: use original garden shot, exact approved Georgian line and original shot length. Compare original/new playback side by side at normal speed, then inspect mouth closures, teeth, jaw, eyes and facial outline. Ask a native Georgian listener to flag every incorrect word; owner judges likeness and movement. Audio waveform correlation only shows audio timing—it does not establish matching mouth shapes. Stop after two failed samples and switch to real filming or voiceover over B-roll.

## Published prices and explicit cost assumptions

These subscriptions are separate from audio bought through Higgsfield. A roughly 500-character script at one credit/character uses 500 credits; three takes × three languages = 4,500 credits, assuming comparable text length and no voice custom-rate multiplier. At full Starter-credit utilization, 1,500 credits for three takes allocates $0.30 per language. Actual cash outlay is $6 for the month; allocation is not an extra invoice. ### Human and hybrid budget scenarios

Published marketplace floors are useful anchors, not Georgian actor quotes: Insense says UGC starts at $100/video; its Brand plan is $500/month billed $1,500 quarterly plus 10% creator payment fee. Ten $100 assets would allocate $1,600/month, $160 each, excluding extra rights, travel and editing. This marketplace is too much fixed cost for the current testing phase. Billo advertises per-video pricing from $99 but full order terms sit behind login, and local Georgian creator availability is not established. [Insense](https://insense.pro/pricing), [Billo](https://billo.app/blog/billo-alternatives/).

Planning assumption, **not a researched local quotation**: existing actor/camera person half-day $150–$400; basic editing of a ten-video batch $100–$300; transport/location $0–$100 = $250–$800, $25–$80 per accepted video if all ten are approved. Three localized editions add native voice/edit review; budget it separately. Equipment, paid actor licensing and site access may push the cost higher. If the actor records usable phone footage without a new fee, incremental cash can be lower, but labor still has value.

Hybrid scenario at ten outputs: same filming/edit assumption $250–$800 plus $89.03 audio+sync plans/usage = $339.03–$889.03, $33.90–$88.90 per accepted video. This includes three lip-correction attempts on 16 seconds each but no newly generated B-roll, paid distribution or full reshoots. Do not present $2 AI usage as a fully produced commercial price.

## Pilot and acceptance rules

Week 1: two concepts per brand, three hooks each = twelve variations, using shared body footage. Create Georgian masters for Pini; Aura language allocation must follow its actual target customer/confirmed product documents, not a default assumption that it has the same market. RU/EN localization after the winning core is understood.

For an original40s hero film keep its narrative; cut separate 20–30s social editions rather than forcing every asset to 40s. Produce 9:16 masters for Reels, 4:5 feed crops and 16:9 website version. The desktop map can appear as a short framed capture; avoid shrinking the whole desktop UI until unreadable. Use deterministic text/logo compositing instead of asking the video generator to spell brand words. Preserve pin geography with direct camera motion, then cut to approved footage.

Gate 1: brand/project claims and exact spoken script approved. Gate 2: each language listened to by native reviewer and owner; brand dictionary includes Pini and თბილისი ბულვარი / Tbilisi Boulevard / Тбилиси Бульвар. Gate 3: 5–8s moving actor sample approved at normal speed. Gate 4: full edit, website spelling, English UI, exact pin zoom and gradual clouds-to-night checked. Gate 5: one final preview, cost ledger and publish approval. No automatic spend after failed gates.

Track: asset ID, concept, hook, language, source/rights, platform, generation attempts, quoted/actual credits, allocated software fee, human time, accepted/rejected reason, UTM, qualified leads and booked viewings. Cost per accepted output = all production costs including rejects / accepted outputs. A rejected output has no meaningful cheap-unit-cost claim.

Primary result: qualified inquiries and booked consultations/viewings. Diagnostic metrics: first3s retention, completion, saves/shares, landing page view rate and inquiry conversion. Hypothesis-only stop rule: pause a variant after it spends twice the agreed allowable qualified-lead cost with zero qualified leads, then inspect creative and landing flow. Allowable lead cost = contribution margin per closed transaction × lead-to-close probability × owner-approved acquisition share; fill with actual business data. Never infer sales from views.

### House and land category hooks

House: “A house is more than its front photograph. Start with where it sits.” Show the actual map category, verified exterior/interior footage and documented property details; compare location/access and usable spaces. Never use generated footage to add a pool, room, view or surrounding street that the property does not have. CTA: open this house's details. Measure qualified house inquiries separately from project inquiries.

Land: “Before you picture a house here, check what this plot actually allows.” Show real map location, actual parcel imagery, documented area/boundaries and accurately attributed planning/access/utilities information. Mark unknowns; do not infer buildability from attractive scenery or generate a future building as existing. CTA: request documented land details. Measure qualified land inquiries and missing-information reports. Native/expert review is needed for technical claims; proposed ad copy is not planning advice.

**Final fixed scope for all options:** Pini map-first projects / houses / land; project units nested, standalone individual flat listings excluded. Aura residential complexes only.
