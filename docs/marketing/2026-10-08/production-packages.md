# Pass 1 — Pini Premium production packages and accepted-asset economics

Research decision draft, 8 October 2026, Asia/Tbilisi. No paid generation, account change, new-service upload or outreach performed. All package prices, labor rates and capacity assumptions are proposals. Vendor prices below are published USD prices checked today, excluding tax; they do not establish Georgian quality or access to the approved Grady voice outside Higgsfield.

## Keep the production premise honest

Preserve the original 40-second actor performance the owner accepted. Approve exact audio and test a 5–8-second correction on original moving footage before promising a full film. Targeted lip correction can alter facial details; neither an audio waveform match nor a vendor marketing claim establishes acceptable lips or likeness. Stop after two failed short auditions and move to filmed speech or continuous voiceover over authentic B-roll.

The scope stays Pini map-first Projects / Houses / Land; standalone flat listings excluded; units nested in project explorers. Aura remains residential complexes. Real category-specific footage and facts are mandatory. Generated property views, added structures, apparent construction progress or assumed buildability cannot be sold as factual media.

## Voice rights and availability: avoid a false Grady transfer promise

Higgsfield's official ownership help permits commercial output use, including client work, while requiring rights in inputs. Its terms prohibit using model outputs to train AI models without the stated exceptions/permission. Reusing an approved audio file in an edit is different from cloning a preset from that audio. **Do not clone Grady from Higgsfield output to recreate it elsewhere.** [Higgsfield ownership help](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/account/who-owns-my-generations-and-can-i-use-them-commercially), [terms](https://higgsfield.ai/terms-of-use-agreement).

ElevenLabs paid-plan audio has commercial-use rights subject to its conditions; free-plan audio does not, and beta-service exclusions apply. Library voices are identified by provider voice IDs and account availability. Some legacy library voices carry custom rate multipliers. A preset called “Grady” inside Higgsfield is **not evidence of the same directly selectable ElevenLabs voice**. Public official searches did not establish a matching external ID. [Commercial use](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform), [available-voice API](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/voices/search), [custom rates](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/26050485079057-What-are-custom-rates-and-credit-multipliers).

Therefore: keep approved Grady recordings; obtain new Grady lines through the known Higgsfield preset only after exact cost preflight. Treat direct ElevenLabs pricing as an alternative voice-route scenario requiring a new audition—not proof that Grady costs $6/month. New-service actor/audio uploads require destination approval. Existing Higgsfield consent does not silently authorize a new platform.

ElevenLabs v3/v4 list KA/RU/EN/HE. Its older Multilingual v2 and Flash v2.5 support RU/EN/HE but omit KA. Model support is not voice-specific pronunciation quality. [Models](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models).

## Pricing inputs and definitions

Sync Creator: $19/month plus usage. At 25 fps, sync-3 costs $0.13340/second. Usage depends on model/frame count; a 30 fps source must be quoted, not priced as 25 fps. ElevenLabs direct Starter: $6/month, 30,000 credits; Creator: $22/month, 121,000 credits. Temporary first-month discounts excluded. [Sync pricing](https://sync.so/pricing), [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing).

* **Master:** an independently scripted/edited 30–40-second body.
* **Variant:** hook/crop/CTA changes reusing a master. Ten variants are not ten new concepts.
* **Language edition:** separately reviewed audio, typography and timing. Ten masters in four languages means forty accepted editions, not ten.
* **Speaking seconds:** only visible presenter footage sent for lip correction; B-roll voiceover does not need lip correction.
* **Attempts:** three charged attempts per unique speaking segment/language in the planning scenario; actual failures can exceed this. Reused, unchanged approved speaking segments do not incur a new generation solely for a different crop.
* **Accepted asset:** passes native speech, owner likeness/movement, facts and final edit checks. Rejected takes remain in cost numerator.

### Retry sensitivity for ten masters, one language

One attempt: $21.34 usage + $25 fees = $46.34. Three attempts: $89.03. Five attempts: $106.72 usage + $25 = $131.72. Extra rounds also require human review/time, which is usually more material than these compute differences. If three failures yield no acceptable output, the $89.03 calculation is not a cost per ten accepted films; production has failed and accepted count is zero.

These software totals exclude capture, editing, native review, research, newly generated B-roll, location/travel, actor/license fees, reshoots, distribution, taxes, sales overhead and unavailable voice acquisition. They assume a reusable legally usable source performance already exists. Exact Grady-through-Higgsfield costs are not covered by this direct-TTS table.

The $900/$1,500 prices are thin if a 30% contribution target is chosen. Floors at that target are approximately $1,021 and $1,663 before sales/overhead—not enough reason to raise prices without testing demand. The $450 offer is viable only as a constrained reuse package; ten wholly different filmed/generated commercials do not fit it.

Capacity: one operator with 100 actual fulfillment hours/month could deliver at most eight $450 orders, four $900 orders or two $1,500 orders under these allowances, before capture coordination/sales/admin. Those counts are capacity illustrations, not demand forecasts. Native reviewers require their own booked capacity and should not be hidden inside “AI does it instantly.”

Suggested hour budgets: $450 script/facts 2h, assembly 5h, variants/export 2h, native QA 1h, consolidated revision 2.5h. $900 scripts/facts 3h, coordination 2h, editing 9h, variants 3h, native QA 2h, consolidated revisions 3h (capture labor belongs to the separate capture quote). $1,500 scripts/facts 4h, coordination 2h, edit 15h, localization/timing 6h, native QA 5h, consolidated revisions 6h.

If existing source footage is only an acceptable generated English master, a new KA localized take must pass the audition before it becomes a sellable capability. If Georgian fails, offer the documented alternative—filmed Georgian presenter, or voiceover over B-roll—with revised scope and price. Do not promise a lip-sync guarantee.

## Optional RU/KA/EN/HE editions and revisions

Additional 30–40-second language edition planning cost: native adaptation 0.75h + review 0.5h + timing/overlay/export 0.75h = 2h/$40; lip usage 16s × 3 × $0.1334 = $6.40; conservative $5 quota/patch allowance = $51.40. An illustrative sell price of $80 gives 35.7% contribution before extra review or reshoot. If a new plan tier is triggered, allocate its incremental fee transparently. An extra language with substantially different duration may require a different cut, not an automatic $80 conversion. Hebrew also requires checked RTL overlays and contact destination.

Included proposed revisions: one consolidated editorial round after script lock; production-caused wrong text/audio or corrupted export corrected as a defect, not sold back as a client revision. Script/property/offer changes after lock, new footage, new languages, added rights or client-requested reshoots require a written scope quote. Technical correction attempts are capped at three in package estimates, but failed quality gates trigger a remedy/re-scope decision rather than delivery of an ugly film.

Rework sensitivity: four extra edit hours cost $80; one additional capture session at the assumed $200 adds $200. Together they consume $280, wiping out the $450 and $900 contributions and leaving only $55.97 on the $1,500 package. Two extra full lip attempts on all ten localized editions add another $42.69. Reshoot terms and an early actor/audio sample are therefore critical.

## Concrete acceptance and ledger

Before work: inventory source footage, release/usage terms, actual frame rate, exact named voice/provider ID, script version, native reviewer, property media/facts and ten-deliverable manifest. Approve script/audio, then short original-footage lip test, then master, then derivatives. Record wall time and failed takes. Cost per accepted edition = all allocated production costs / accepted editions; also report cost per unique master so cheap derivatives cannot disguise production inefficiency.

Archive approved performance and exact narration locally with project/version identifiers. Commercial output rights do not guarantee a voice will stay available for future synthesis. Maintain a fallback recorded actor or separately approved alternative voice; no unapproved voice substitution, voice-model export or preset cloning.
