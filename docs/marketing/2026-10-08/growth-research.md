# Pini + Aura growth research — 8 October 2026

Research specialist memo. No campaigns, paid media, messages, code changes or publication performed. Sources reviewed 8 October 2026. All budgets, funnel rates and experiment gates below are planning assumptions, not measured Pini/Aura performance. No private analytics were available.

## Product facts and recommended brand relationship

Aura's confirmed scope is residential building complexes located in Georgia, targeting buyers in Georgia, Russia and Israel. It has Georgian/Russian/Hebrew/English locales, anonymous browsing and independent CRM/inventory. It is not an Israeli-property business. Pini's role is map-first discovery of Georgian developments. The owner approved Aura's longer cinematic editorial homepage in D-231 on 7 October; do not reopen that decision. The two brands may share a marketing production team and licensed asset library; they should retain distinct customer promises, accounts, lead ownership and conversion reporting.

Recommendation: Pini = 'See where your next home could be'; Aura = 'Choose your Georgian home with a person who knows it.' These are proposed copy directions. Pini makes the market legible; Aura earns trust through named experts, verified project information and specific buying support. Shared ownership is not a reason to claim Pini is neutral while secretly privileging Aura: any commercial referrals or sponsored prominence should be visible and agreed. No shared pixels, CRM exports or automatic cross-brand lead transfer assumed. Cooperation starts with independently published neighborhood research, jointly licensed footage, and manually approved public links. A public user choosing to contact Aura can submit a new inquiry explicitly on Aura; do not silently copy a Pini record.

### Days 15–30: controlled first experiment

## Scenario economics (USD; assumptions, not quotations)

Formula: impressions = spend/CPM*1,000; clicks = impressions*CTR; inquiries = clicks*landing inquiry rate; qualified conversations = inquiries*qualification rate. No forecast sale conversion supplied as fact.

For a $1,000 media test:
- Conservative: $12CPM,0.7%CTR,2%inquiry conversion,30%qualified =83,333 impressions,583 clicks,11.7 inquiries,3.5 qualified conversations; ~$286/qualified.
- Working hypothesis: $8CPM,1%CTR,3%inquiry conversion,40%qualified =125,000 impressions,1,250 clicks,37.5 inquiries,15 qualified; ~$66.67/qualified.
- Strong hypothetical: $5CPM,1.4%CTR,5%inquiry conversion,50%qualified =200,000 impressions,2,800 clicks,140 inquiries,70 qualified; ~$14.29/qualified.

These deliberately wide outcomes show why a $1,000 test buys learning, not guaranteed sales. Raw impressions may repeat individuals. Agency/developer fees unknown; staff default commissions are not Aura's revenue. Before scaling, obtain gross fee, variable fulfillment cost, staff commission, cancellations/refunds and close rate. Example only: $100,000 sale at hypothetical3%agency revenue=$3,000; $1,500staff/fulfillment costs leave$1,500contribution. If20%ofqualifiedconversations close and30%contribution allocatedtoacquisition, maximum CPQC=$90. At5%close maximum=$22.50. Change assumptions to actual signed contracts. Pini developer subscription/lead fee business model not confirmed; do not assign Aura commission economics to it.
