# Choose a business through bounded pilots

Proposal, 8 October 2026. This does not authorize outreach, publication, spending or implementation. The owner chooses an offer and budget first. Small samples identify operating problems and customer objections; they do not establish market-wide conversion rates.

## Common preparation

Keep Pini map-first with Projects / Houses / Land; apartment exploration stays nested inside projects. Project records are free. Do not advertise house/land pricing until decided. Aura independently represents residential complexes and retains its approved cinematic design.

Before any paid pilot, record current supply, real inquiry recipient, approved facts/media, exact deliverable, revision limit, variable cost and available fulfillment hours. Public test labels, ambiguous currency and wrong-language destinations fail this gate. Avoid simultaneous redesign, new voice, new offer and new audience: their combined outcome cannot diagnose the cause.

## Three comparable learning tests

| | A: project spotlight | B: assistance | C: production |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer assumption | Developers will pay for relevant discovery once Pini has useful traffic | Buyers need a defined service beyond information/agency help already available | Project owners will pay enough for reliable approved content |
| Small initial cohort | Three project owners, one district/category wedge | Five buyers with actual project interest, one language initially | Two project owners, same narrow production scope |
| Deliverable to approve | One dated, clearly sponsored placement with a report | Written scope, named advisor and agreed next step | One master plus a stated number of reused variants; localization separate |
| Primary evidence | Paid acceptance, delivery, relevant connections and renewal | Paid acceptance if testing a fee, completed service and net contribution | Paid acceptance, accepted assets, actual hours and contribution |
| Failure to diagnose | Little relevant audience, unclear placement value or slow recipient response | Duplicate free agency service, weak value or excessive advisor time | Rejected face/speech, repeated revisions or scope exceeding price |
| Next decision | Improve discovery/offer or repeat the same scope; do not scale empty exposure | Separate Aura agency help from any distinct Pini paid service | Reduce scope, raise price or improve workflow before more orders |

Counts are proposed learning cohorts, not revenue forecasts. A complimentary trial can demonstrate usefulness but must be labeled separately from evidence that anyone pays. Do not invent customer testimonials or a case study from internal footage.

## Record results consistently

One pilot ledger row per customer/order: brand, offer version, category, language, permission status, quoted price, cash received, discounts/refunds, deliverable, deadline, hours by role, vendor usage, capture/licensing costs, rejection/revision reasons, accepted date, recipient response, relevant next action and repeat purchase/renewal. Use anonymous IDs in the marketing report; private contact data stays in the approved operational system.

Calculate contribution as collected revenue minus refunds and direct fulfillment costs, including internal labor at an explicit planning rate. Calculate acquisition costs separately, then show what remains after acquisition. Revenue from an agency sale is the actual agency fee, not the property's transaction price. Pipeline is not cash.

For C, variants are cheaper because they reuse an approved body; a new body is a new master. Another language is another edition with its own audio, lip and native review. An asset rejected for incorrect speech is not an accepted output, even if the generation completed successfully. Track first-pass acceptance and total attempts per accepted asset.

## Attention experiment without a viral promise

Use two approved hook concepts with the same body, language, destination and CTA. First review factual clarity, speech and the first three seconds through moderated viewing. After release authorization, compare exposure, retention and relevant destination actions. Record platform, placement, date and spend; do not pool Georgian, Russian, English and Hebrew into one winner.

Useful hook proposals: “What is actually available at this pin?” and “Three things to check before choosing this project.” The map-to-place transition can remain a brand signature. Straight zoom into the named pin, real English/Georgian interface appropriate to the edition, continuous approved voiceover and gradual cloud/day-to-night movement carry forward the owner's film notes.

Views are attention; repeat exploration, qualified conversations and accepted paid orders are different evidence. A hook with many views and poor fit should not automatically get more budget. Check recipient response and page clarity before attributing weak leads to the creative.

## Make each weekly decision explicit

At the agreed review date classify the result as **continue unchanged**, **revise one assumption**, **stop this offer**, or **insufficient evidence**. Record the numbers and the reason. Missing data or a low-volume cohort is insufficient evidence, not proof of success or failure.

For A, do not sell guaranteed leads before observed capacity and audience support the claim. For B, do not hide agency representation or imply every Pini inquiry belongs to Aura. For C, do not sell a scope with negative contribution or fulfillment hours above the team's available capacity. These are operating gates, not statistical significance thresholds.

The [measurement specification](measurement.md) provides event definitions and sensitivity calculations. Keep the planned spend-pause rule separate from an experiment conclusion: stopping after a cash limit manages risk; it does not prove a creative cannot work.

## Morning choice sheet

1. Select A, B or C as the first Pini Premium hypothesis; the free discovery foundation remains common.
2. State the exact paid customer, deliverable, language and exclusions.
3. Set the pilot ceiling for cash and human hours, separately from a 90-day growth envelope.
4. Confirm who owns supply refresh, buyer response, production approval and the weekly ledger.
5. Choose a review date and an outcome that would justify another cohort.

Prepare the selected brief and design for review before implementation. The other plans remain alternatives, not parallel launches. No source-code change is needed to make this business choice.
