# Brief and execution agreement

## Objective

Produce three complete, evidence-backed marketing plans for Pini and Aura that the owner can compare in the morning. Each includes positioning, audience, channels, repeatable attention mechanisms, website design proposals, Premium economics, UGC costs, exact steps and measurable acceptance criteria.

## Confirmed owner decisions, 8 October 2026

1. This agent orchestrates marketing research for both brands with three specialist agents.
2. All work is local; no pushes, publication or deployment. Any code changes belong on a separate local branch and require a detailed explanation.
3. Pini remains map-first. New-building projects, houses and land remain supported categories. Individual flat listings are excluded. Nested apartment inventory inside a new-building project is allowed.
4. Project listings are free to grow Pini's supply and brand. Pini Premium is intended; its beneficiary, deliverables and price remain undecided.
5. Prepare several complete alternatives and challenge assumptions where evidence warrants it.
6. Existing video: original roughly 40-second version's face, movement and lips were preferred; bad speech was the principal defect. Later still-to-video remake was rejected. Preserve the accepted performance as the next repair reference.

### Superseded interpretations

The temporary interpretation that houses and land were excluded is superseded by the owner's explicit correction. “Only new buildings” describes Pini's apartment/project offering, not a removal of houses or land. Earlier SaaS subscription assumptions do not override the confirmed free-project policy. The house/land pricing policy is still unspecified.

## Existing Aura decisions retained

Aura represents residential complexes in Georgia and targets Georgia/Russia/Israel through ka/ru/he/en. It is independent of Pini, with its own CRM and permissions. D-231 approved the longer cinematic editorial homepage. No public customer registration or new catalogue filters are introduced. Production launch prerequisites still require current verification; local QA is not production readiness.

## Team ownership

- Growth specialist: market, competitors, audience, supply acquisition, business models and 90-day economics.
- Design specialist: current public Pini inspection, existing Aura design evidence, map-first design alternatives and conversion experiments.
- UGC specialist: production reliability, vendor prices, accepted-output costs, speech/lip quality and creative concepts.
- Orchestrator: scope reconciliation, independent evidence review, arithmetic, integrated plans and change log.

## Execution sequence

1. Establish current scope, branch and evidence boundaries.
2. Research independent specialist questions in parallel.
3. Integrate findings and resolve contradictions against latest owner decisions.
4. Verify consequential factual and pricing claims independently.
5. Write comparable full options and a dependency-ordered backlog.
6. Refine overnight through finite scheduled follow-ups; record material changes.
7. Morning review: owner chooses the plan and budget; production releases remain separate actions.

## Non-goals

No paid video tests, new vendor uploads, ad spend, bulk lead exports, messages, live account/settings changes, pricing publication, logo/brand renaming, source-code redesign or Pini runtime changes. No invented market share, conversion uplift, inventory, construction status, guaranteed returns or claims of exclusive innovation.

## Acceptance scenarios

- All three options preserve map-first Projects/Houses/Land and exclude standalone flat listings.
- Each explains how both brands work independently and where shared production can help.
- Every material external statistic has a source, date, geography and relevant limitation.
- Prices distinguish vendor usage, subscription cash, human labor, retries, paid distribution and accepted outputs.
- Proposed Premium prices and budgets are not presented as quotes or commitments.
- Website recommendations preserve Aura's selected design and distinguish live observation from concept.
- Each action has an owner, dependency, measurement and decision gate.
- Final repository diff contains only research deliverables unless an individually justified code change is recorded.
