# One map, three category launch tracks

Proposal, 8 October 2026. This fills an execution gap: the project-led launch must not quietly turn houses and land into decorative tabs. All categories remain available within Pini's map-first identity. These are separate operating tracks, not a new flat-listing catalogue. Aura's business remains residential complexes.

## Distinct minimum useful records

| Category | Minimum buyer facts to source | Spatial presentation | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| New-building project | Project identity, developer/authorized representative, dated construction and sales status, approved images, price/currency/basis or request-price, actual contact | Project pin; licensed masterplan and nested building/floor/unit explorer when source data exists | Open project; choose nested apartment or contact actual recipient |
| House | House identity, actual condition/photo date, interior/total area basis, plot area separately, asking currency/basis, availability and representative | House pin with stated precision; location detail appropriate to approved data | View house; ask the authorized recipient about that house |
| Land | Parcel identity/reference where authorized, location precision, sourced boundary if available, land area, asking currency/basis, dated access/utilities/use information with unknowns | Parcel pin; outline only with reliable source, otherwise a location point clearly identified as such | View land; ask about this parcel and the missing facts |

Minimum means enough honest information to understand the next action, not every imaginable field. Missing optional facts remain Unknown. A public parcel reference is not a legal opinion; a map outline is not proof of title, permitted use or buildability. No private authority documents or personal seller data belong in marketing reports.

## First two weeks: prepare each category

**Days 1–3:** supply lead counts real authorized records by category, area, supplier and freshness. Identify unique properties and duplicates. Record known buyer-contact recipients and media rights. Keep researched prospects separate from publishable supply. Owner decides house/land listing terms; until then do not promise free posting for those categories.

**Days 4–7:** content lead produces one category-specific fact sheet and draft detail-card example for each category using approved data. Design researcher tests Projects → nested apartment, Houses → house/plot area, and Land → boundary/unknown-use comprehension. A land card must not reuse a project floor selector. Do not invent missing property photos or diagrams to make the examples look complete.

**Days 8–14:** choose the first paid-distribution category/area only after its destination is useful. Keep the other categories discoverable and honest. Prepare a separate content and supplier-research backlog for each. A category with no authorized records receives an empty-state concept and research work, not fabricated inventory or ad spend.

## Density and sparse-map behavior

Agree what a result counts before onboarding: one project is one development, with apartments nested and excluded from the map-listing total. Proposed Houses counts are distinct house records; a community group marker is navigation, not an extra house. Proposed Land counts are distinct parcel records; a plot-development overview is not another parcel. If only an overview is supplied, label it as such and do not assert individual stock. Cross-category references use shared source identities to prevent duplicate inventory. These rules await owner choice.

Paper acceptance example: a project with 188 nested flats, a community with four distinct house records and a development with three distinct parcel records should yield one project, four houses and three parcels. Community/development group markers add zero stock. Without individual records, the latter counts remain unknown rather than inferred from marketing images.

The initial project-cluster hypothesis is at least five complete authorized records from three suppliers. This is a readiness proposal, not a universal threshold. No comparable house/land count is established: use observed task usefulness and real record quality rather than copying the project number.

When a selected area/category has no results, state `No listed land in this area yet` or equivalent category copy, retain map/category context, and offer a deliberate wider-area action. Do not silently switch land to projects or relocate to another city. A wider search previews the area change; Back restores the prior category and extent. A text result fallback retains the same category and filters if map tiles fail.

Search scope and viewport must be distinguishable: moving the map should not silently imply that all records across Georgia disappeared. Propose an explicit `Search this area` behavior for owner review. Counts describe the actual active scope. Design choice remains pending; no new behavior was implemented.

Use named wider-area actions such as `Show Land in wider Tbilisi`, only with a known broader result count. Avoid `Nearby` until its distance/area basis is defined. Keep the Lisi subcluster separate from the western project corridor; neither proves walking or commute proximity.

## Fact-specific freshness

Track and label availability confirmation, price confirmation, photo taken/supplied date and location precision separately where known. An edited title or a new photo must not refresh an old price. `Not supplied` or `Not recently confirmed` is more accurate than zero or a blanket verification badge. Describe an approximate location, supplier-provided outline or documented boundary at its actual evidence level; private authority records stay private. Proposed labels require source and owner review before implementation.

Paper acceptance: current house photos with an old price must show separate dates. A land marketing outline without supporting boundary documentation must remain identified as a marketing outline. Neither example permits invented condition, availability or buildability claims.

## Growth by category

| Track | First useful series | Supplier research | Evidence for expansion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Projects | One pin, three dated project facts; real explorer demonstration | Developers with current stock and update owners | Complete records, current nested inventory, useful recipient response and repeat exploration |
| Houses | House area versus plot area; actual condition versus architectural rendering | Authorized house sellers/developers; avoid scraped private contacts | Buyers correctly understand areas/condition and reach a real house recipient |
| Land | What is documented, what is unknown; sourced location and access explanation | Authorized parcel representatives with fact/media provenance | Buyers understand boundaries/unknowns and inquiries can be answered without buildability guesses |

No equal spending requirement: allocate effort where the destination and response operation are ready. Different readiness dates do not remove categories. House/land inquiries do not route automatically to Aura; any future service expansion needs a separate owner decision.

## Weekly category scorecard

Report unique authorized records, supplier concentration, missing facts, freshness, empty-result share, detail opens, accepted inquiries, qualified connections and recipient response by category and area. Do not combine a large project count with a nearly empty land category into a reassuring national total. Units inside projects are a separate inventory measure, not additional standalone property listings.

Before expanding geography, review update workload and useful buyer tasks. Before monetizing house/land posting or promotion, agree its customer, terms and deliverable separately. Project listing remains free regardless of Premium selection.

## Proposed acceptance tasks

1. Start on Projects, open a development, select a nested apartment, and return to the same map.
2. Switch to Houses and explain which figure is house area and which is plot area.
3. Open Land, explain whether the shape is a sourced parcel boundary or location point, and identify an Unknown fact.
4. Reach a zero-result area, broaden deliberately and return without category or city changing unexpectedly.
5. Identify the actual inquiry recipient in every category; explain why a Pini land inquiry does not become an Aura agency lead automatically.

These tasks supplement [screen briefs](screen-briefs.md) and [supply onboarding](supply-density.md). They are proposed research, not completed user testing.
