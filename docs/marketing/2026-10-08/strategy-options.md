# Three complete growth plans for Pini and Aura

Decision draft, 8 October 2026. Every package price, budget, volume target and funnel rate below is a hypothesis for discussion—not a quote, forecast or authorized spend. Sources and evidence limits are in the three specialist reports.

## The decision

Choose the first customer problem to monetize. Keep the free Pini discovery product useful in every option. Aura remains a separate agency, not a disguised destination for every Pini inquiry.

| | Plan A: useful local marketplace | Plan B: trusted buying journey | Plan C: property media business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main Pini advantage | Dense, current map supply | Clear comparison and optional human guidance | Useful map plus distinctive project storytelling |
| Premium buyer | Developer/project owner buying promotion | Buyer buying a defined assistance service, if validated | Developer/project owner buying content production |
| Aura role | Independent agency among transparently identified participants | Principal agency brand for represented complexes | Separate internal client and proof of production quality |
| First 90-day planning envelope | $6,000 | $9,000 | $5,500 |
| First evidence of viability | Repeat discovery and paid promotion renewal | Qualified conversations, completed viewings, contribution economics | Paid pilots, acceptable assets and profitable repeat production |
| Biggest risk | Paid visibility has little value before buyer attention exists | Pini duplicates Aura's service and confuses representation | A studio earns revenue without building a durable marketplace |

All options preserve map-first Projects / Houses / Land. No standalone flat listings. Project units stay inside a development's building/floor explorer. House and land detail flows use category-specific facts, not apartment diagrams. Free project listing does not yet establish free house/land pricing.

## What I would challenge

**“There is no good product” is too strong.** Korter already provides project maps, development cards and multilingual discovery; ss.ge serves a broad property market. Pini should win a specific task rather than claim the category is empty. Proposed task: a buyer can understand where a property is, what is actually available and who can answer the next question with less confusion. [Korter](https://korter.ge/en/new-projects-in-tbilisi-on-map), [ss.ge](https://home.ss.ge/en/real-estate).

**Free listings are an acquisition tool, not a moat.** If free produces stale pins and incomplete information, it weakens the brand. Define a listing review scope, information owner and refresh procedure. “Verified” must describe the actual checks; it must not imply title, construction or investment guarantees.

Overnight verification: Korter itself already offers free building/cottage project listings and promotion services. Pini therefore needs a measured experience/data/onboarding advantage beyond free pricing. The [viability memo](competitive-position-and-viability.md) also shows that the proposed low-priced packages would need more paying customers than the initial pilots to cover their planning envelopes.

**A huge national brand starts with a useful local result.** Preserve the national map, but recruit dense supply in a reachable area first. Keep all supported categories visible; avoid spending equally on three thin catalogues before proving buyer connections. Choose the first district/category based on actual inventory, not this report's preference.

**Viral attention cannot substitute for purchasing intent.** Design memorable series and measure their business path. A spectacular balcony clip that produces no relevant project visits or consultations is a creative learning result, not a growth success.

**AI production price is not accepted-video price.** The original good-looking footage is valuable. Repeatedly regenerating the face to repair speech has already failed. Preserve footage, approve audio first and audition a short mouth correction before a full revision.

## Plan A — useful local marketplace

### Positioning and product

Pini: **Find the place. Understand the property. Make the next connection.** The map is the home screen and recognizable brand asset. Three category chips lead to project, house or land pins and category-specific cards. New-building projects open a masterplan and nested apartment explorer. Honest availability and prices, clear contact recipients and readable cards are the value.

Aura: **A personal agency for selected residential developments in Georgia.** It keeps its cinematic editorial identity, represented projects and multilingual advisors. Pini's paid prominence and any agency relationship are disclosed; no automatic routing of unrelated inquiries to Aura.

### Premium offer to test

Free project record includes useful basic information and an honest contact path. Proposed Premium is a time-bounded, clearly sponsored project spotlight with defined creative/placement/reporting deliverables. Price hypotheses: $99 / $149 / $249 monthly, with different scope. Advertising spend is separate. No guaranteed lead volume, paid “trust” badge or hidden removal of useful free information.

Example economics: $149 revenue minus $60 direct service cost gives $89 contribution before platform, acquisition, tax and overhead. Ten customers contribute $890/month—not enough to justify a large team. Track support time and renewal before expanding. A house/land promotion offer needs separate validation rather than inheriting project pricing.

### Acquisition and attention

- Supply: research 30 relevant developers/project owners in the chosen wedge; prepare a free listing invitation and assisted onboarding pack. After the owner approves outreach, invite the first ten, learn missing-data friction and adjust.
- Buyers: Georgian-first original map demonstrations, district comparisons and project-specific search. Use Facebook/Instagram as paid hypotheses; TikTok/Shorts initially for organic discovery pending paid-market eligibility.
- Recurring series: **One pin, three things to know.** Open with a useful geographic surprise, reveal three dated facts, then show the real map destination. Separate house/land episodes use their own facts.
- Aura content: advisor explains one practical project decision and links to a represented complex, with a single consultation CTA.

### Website design

Pini direction: **map utility**. Keep cream/pastel map identity, named city shortcuts where supply exists, synchronized image cards, explicit currency and category-appropriate price basis, and a mobile card drawer. Show derived per-m² prices only where source amount/area supports them. A text alternative supports accessibility; it does not replace the map-first identity. Separate “List your project — free” from buyer assistance.

Aura direction: **cinematic agency with concrete proof**. Preserve the approved longer homepage and motion. Improve the factual agency subline, project-entry links, real advisor profiles and source-dated information. Do not shorten the site simply because a generic conversion pattern says so.

### 90-day steps and decision gates

| Period | Work | Evidence to continue |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Audit supply, correct public facts, draft free onboarding, observe five buyer tasks | Users understand categories, price and next contact; valid recipient for every promoted listing |
| Days 15–30 | Reach proposed 10–15 complete projects in a useful wedge; publish drafts only after approval; test three hooks per two bodies | Proposed 80% completeness and 90% refreshed within 30 days; actual exploration and response baseline |
| Days 31–60 | One adjacent district if supply is useful; pilot three sponsored packages | Deliverables fulfilled, relevant buyer actions, support time and margin recorded |
| Days 61–90 | Expand toward proposed 30–50 projects only if update capacity allows; test ten paying customers | Renewal intent/actual renewals, contribution, buyer trust and repeat exploration |

Targets are not existing inventory or promises. Do not buy substantial traffic into empty category/area states.

### 90-day budget hypothesis

Supply onboarding $1,200; refresh labor $600; content $1,500; media $1,800; tools/research $300; contingency $600 = **$6,000**. Internal time is an economic allowance, not necessarily an external invoice. Revenue should not be netted against this envelope until paid pilots exist.

### Metrics and stop rules

Pini: complete records by district/category, empty-result rate, qualified connections per 100 landing visitors, recipient response and repeat project exploration. Premium: delivered scope, margin and renewal. Aura: qualified consultation and completed viewing. Stop scaling traffic when facts, recipients or response capacity fail; stop selling promotion if relevant audience cannot be demonstrated.

## Plan B — trusted buying journey

### Positioning and product

Pini's map helps buyers discover and understand options; education answers uncertainty. Aura's named experts provide agency assistance for represented residential complexes. Houses and land remain Pini categories and must not be silently handed to an agency whose current scope excludes them.

### Premium offer to test

Optional Pini buyer assistance could mean a documented shortlist or a bounded consultation. It is a separate paid service hypothesis—not a fee to see prices or contact details. Proposed $99 package: two expert hours at $20/hour plus $10 administration leaves $49 before acquisition, refunds and overhead. At $40 acquisition cost only $9 remains. This is fragile.

Alternative within this plan: Pini stays free and clearly links to **assistance by Aura** where relevant and explicitly chosen. Aura earns through its actual agency contracts. I prefer this alternative over selling overlapping advice twice, unless paid Pini assistance offers a distinct benefit. No contract fee is assumed here.

### Acquisition and attention

- Georgia first; focused Hebrew/Israel testing because surveyed new-development demand includes Israeli buyers. Russian-language audiences are segmented by actual eligible geography, not assumed Russia delivery.
- Advisor-led content: **One property, one decision**; explain a real layout, location or construction-information tradeoff.
- Useful tension: “The view is beautiful. What does the floor plan change?” or “What this rendering cannot tell you.” Use factual answers rather than fear or promised returns.
- Create a current project dossier: location, construction status, renders versus current photos, availability and exact next step. Locale-specific landing pages match the ad language.

### Website design

Pini: **concierge map** with optional “Tell us what you need” beside the visible map and category controls. Basic facts remain accessible without form/chat gating. House/land questions differ from project-unit preferences.

Aura: **advisor-led credibility within the cinematic site**. Keep approved architecture imagery, add actual advisors and language capabilities where verified, clarify representation and strengthen the path from project to inquiry. Four reviewed locales and Hebrew RTL remain required; language quality is not cosmetic.

### 90-day steps and decision gates

| Period | Work | Evidence to continue |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Confirm 2–3 represented complexes and agents, reviewed facts, contact flow and production prerequisites | Agency mandate, current inventory, real contact and production readiness verified |
| Days 15–30 | Draft six evidence-led videos; interview ten buyers across priority languages; test assistance scope | Buyer pain and willingness to pay for a specific service, not generic “Premium” interest |
| Days 31–60 | Two audience/language campaign cells after release approval; collect conversation and viewing outcomes | Lead quality and human follow-up, not cheap contact clicks |
| Days 61–90 | Improve follow-up, publish consented case evidence if available, pilot one service | Contribution economics and actual viewing/cohort progression |

### 90-day budget hypothesis

Media $4,500; capture/edit/localization $2,500; research/testing $500; tools $300; contingency $1,200 = **$9,000**. Agency fulfillment and project-data operations are additional internal costs to measure.

### Economics and stop rules

Illustration only: $100,000 sale × hypothetical 3% agency fee = $3,000 revenue; minus $1,500 staff/fulfillment = $1,500 contribution. At 10% qualified-conversation close rate and a 30% acquisition allowance, maximum cost per qualified conversation is $45. Actual signed fees and observed close rates must replace these inputs. No guaranteed sales follow from a media budget.

Stop scaling when agents cannot answer, inventory cannot fulfill interest or contribution-based acquisition limits are exceeded. Keep Pini and Aura cohorts separate; an outbound Aura click is not an Aura accepted lead.

## Plan C — property media business

### Positioning and product

Pini remains a useful free-project map with houses/land, while Premium sells content production to developers/project owners. Aura is an independent client of the shared production team. Good work for Aura proves production quality, not external willingness to pay.

### Premium offer to test

Bounded packages: proposed $450 basic project edit; $900 original capture with selected localization; $1,500 monthly production package. Define outputs, formats, rights, revision count and turnaround. Ad budgets are separate; listing is not conditional on buying content.

Initial coarse $900 illustration: capture $200 + editing $200 + language/QA $100 + tools/retries $100 = $600 direct cost and $300 contribution (33.3%). Three packages/month give $900 contribution. An additional $300 of rework eliminates margin. Overnight refinement scopes the package as three masters plus seven reused variants and costs it at about $714.21, leaving only 20.6% contribution under the stated labor assumptions. The detailed [production package model](production-packages.md) supersedes the coarse estimate for scope-specific pricing decisions. Quoted local production costs and measured revision time are essential before publishing prices.

### Acquisition and attention

- Developer-facing portfolio: real map-to-place ad, original advisor/presenter footage and a factual render/site comparison. No fake client outcomes.
- Buyer series: **From pin to place**, using a recognizable direct zoom and authentic location footage. Make separate brand masters with separate CTAs.
- Behind-the-scenes proof: exact approved narration, source images, current facts and what was filmed/generated. This differentiates a production service through reliability rather than promising magic AI.
- House/land content can follow once legitimate assets and category facts exist; never fabricate a house on a plot or imply buildability from appearance.

### Website design

Pini: **editorial map**. The map remains the product; location stories open around real pins and link to category-appropriate details. A Premium page explains scoped production examples and price hypotheses only after approval. Sponsored/client work is labeled.

Aura: cinematic editorial collection, with advisor context and genuine represented project evidence. Shared production standards do not require a shared visual identity.

### 90-day steps and decision gates

| Period | Work | Evidence to continue |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Inventory original accepted footage; create a repair brief; obtain quotes after outreach approval | Exact audio and original performance quality reference established |
| Days 15–30 | One short lip-correction audition, then one 40-second portfolio master and three cutdowns if approved | Native speech and owner likeness/movement approval; full cost ledger |
| Days 31–60 | Seek two paid pilots after outreach approval; fixed output/revision terms | Actual customer payment, delivered scope, labor/rework recorded |
| Days 61–90 | Aim for 3–5 repeatable packages/month only if profitable | Repeat demand, predictable margin and turnaround; ongoing map utility not neglected |

The proposed paid test is a future gate, not an action taken by this report. A new-service actor/audio upload requires specific destination authorization before use.

### 90-day budget hypothesis

Portfolio/capture $1,500; native QA/edit $1,000; prospecting/research $500; buyer media $1,000; tools/retries $500; contingency $1,000 = **$5,500**.

### Metrics and stop rules

Cost per accepted asset including rejected takes; revision hours; native speech approval; owner face/movement approval; pilot payment and repeat rate; contribution margin. Stop after two failed short technical auditions and change the production method. Do not make a full-film retry the quality test.

## UGC cost decision across all plans

Use original/human performance, real interface captures and approved project media as the core. Targeted lip correction on original footage is a candidate workflow; facial detail preservation and Georgian quality must pass visual/listening review. No mouth-only editing boundary is guaranteed. Sync's published Creator plan is $19/month plus usage; sync-3 at 25 fps is $0.1334/second. ElevenLabs Starter's regular price is $6/month; temporary discounts are excluded from recurring budgets. [Sync pricing](https://sync.so/pricing), [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing).

Ten accepted videos with 16 speaking seconds each and three attempts allocate $64.03 lip-correction usage + $19 Sync + $6 audio = **$89.03/month, $8.90/video**, excluding filming, editing, native review and distribution. One video under the same assumptions costs **$31.40** because the subscriptions cannot be spread. At higher frame rates, re-quote the usage rate.

This is a direct-provider **alternative voice** scenario, not a quote for the approved Grady preset through Higgsfield. Matching external Grady voice identity/access has not been established. Keep approved recordings, and obtain exact Higgsfield quotes for new Grady lines. Do not clone the preset from generated output. Ten independent masters in four languages are forty editions; the detailed model allocates $297.13 software under its assumptions before human production/review.

Local filming/editing assumption, not a quote: ten videos $250–$800. Adding the software scenario gives **$339.03–$889.03, $33.90–$88.90 each** if ten outputs are accepted. No implication that these include three language editions, reshoots, agency fees or a fresh AI-generated master. See the UGC report for boundaries and alternatives.

Historical Higgsfield allocation: $50 / about 1,000 credits = about $0.05/credit. The recorded 864-credit experiment included 384 credits of tests and 480 for the main film plus ending; approximately $43.20 total, with $24 attributable to the output-generation portion. This is not the current plan price or a new quote.

## The recommendation I would defend

Choose **Plan A as Pini's foundation and Plan B's agency approach for Aura**. Pilot Plan C only as a bounded Premium service once production quality and willingness to pay are demonstrated. Do not launch three Premium promises at once.

For the first two weeks: fix factual presentation, establish dense/current supply, test buyer comprehension and create one reliable production reference. This protects the brands before asking the market for attention or money.

Morning choices: which single Premium problem to test; first geography/category wedge; available 90-day budget; the explicit public relationship between the two brands. These choices should precede broad implementation.
