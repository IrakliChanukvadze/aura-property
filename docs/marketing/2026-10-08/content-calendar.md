# First four weeks of content — draft calendar

Proposed after factual, production and publication gates pass. This is a production plan, not a publishing schedule or claim that assets exist. Native-review every spoken/visible language. Start with fewer strong masters; cut alternative hooks from the same body instead of regenerating complete videos.

## Week 2: turn attention into useful questions

Pini: one timed neighborhood route; one house-location episode; one land-facts episode. Record time/day/route rather than promising an invariant commute. Land content explains supplied access, boundaries or documented use; it does not infer buildability.

Aura: one render-versus-current-site explanation; one advisor answer to a genuine buyer question; one exact apartment-explorer demonstration inside a represented complex. No fake personal purchase story.

Three hook variants per two strongest bodies: geographic surprise, useful question, direct comparison. Keep body/destination constant so the opening is the main changed variable. Review saves/shares, destination visits and qualified conversations together.

## Week 3: repeat the winning format

Pini: two “One pin, three things to know” episodes in the same useful wedge; one free-onboarding question answered publicly. Aura: two “One property, one decision” episodes and one language-specific advisor answer. Localize selected winners rather than multiplying every weak concept into four languages.

## Week 4: publish evidence and test a business offer

Pini: dated information update and clear explanation of how sponsored promotion differs from ordinary discovery, if Plan A selected. If Plan C selected, show a production example with scoped output and actual cost—not invented client ROI. If Plan B selected, explain the precise optional assistance benefit and who provides it.

Aura: a real, consented client example only if one exists; otherwise a clearly framed educational scenario, not a testimonial. Review all inquiries with sales staff and make the next month's content answer the recurring objections.

## Brand and voice consistency

- Reuse a consented recognizable adult presenter; preserve actual performance where possible.
- Lock project names, pronunciation and fact sheets. Georgian, Russian, English and Hebrew content receive appropriate native review.
- Pini visual signature: map, pin, direct movement to place. Aura visual signature: warm cinematic architecture and named human guidance.
- Use deterministic overlays for brand/website spelling. Generated video should not be trusted to spell the interface.
- Produce 9:16 social editions and 16:9 website master with readable text/safe zones. Do not squeeze a full desktop website into a tiny vertical frame.
- Record failed takes and labor in the cost ledger; stop repeating a failed full-film method.

## Outreach drafts — not sent

### Free project onboarding

“We are building Pini's map of new-building projects in Georgia. Project listings are free. We would like to present [project] with accurate location, current project information and a clear buyer contact path. Could your authorized representative review the fact/media checklist and tell us who owns updates? Optional Premium services are separate; listing does not require purchasing them.”

Use only after terms and outreach are approved. Do not promise audience size, instant publication or a verification guarantee.

### Production quote

“Please quote a half-day session with our consenting adult presenter and ten accepted 25–40-second marketing edits. Separate capture, editing, travel/location, native language review, revisions, paid advertising usage rights and additional language versions. We will supply licensed project material and approved scripts. Please state what requires a reshoot and what is included in each revision round.”

### Specific Premium research question

“Would this exact package solve a problem you currently pay to solve: [deliverables, duration, limits and proposed price]? What would you remove, add or refuse to pay for? Which current expense would it replace?”

An interview answer is weaker than an actual paid pilot. Do not count hypothetical enthusiasm as revenue validation.
