# Business rules
Last updated: 2026-10-06

## Confirmed scope
BR-001 Aura Property is a real estate agency working only with residential building complexes, not individual houses.
BR-002 All represented complexes are currently in Georgia. Buyer target markets are Georgia, Russia, and Israel.
BR-003 Public website languages: Georgian (ka), Russian (ru), Hebrew (he), English (en). Hebrew requires RTL support. Translation completeness, fallback, default language, and admin language are open.
BR-004 Public scope includes agency information, current complexes, interactive exploration, inquiries, contact by phone/WhatsApp, and blog listing/article pages. Detailed content and publishing workflow remain open.
BR-005 Aura is standalone with independent data/admin. Reuse Pini code for complex uploads, explorer, and complex/apartment inquiries; no runtime integration or shared live inventory. Future relationships are out of MVP scope.
BR-006 Explorer logic: complex -> building/tower -> selectable floor on cover image -> floor plan -> selectable apartment -> detail/inquiry. Preserve image-based hover interactions, with usable tap/keyboard alternatives. Aura layout/UI can differ. Sold/reserved browsing policy remains open.

## Inquiry baseline agreed by reference
BR-007 Reuse Pini's current complex/apartment inquiry inputs and behavior; adapt UI for Aura.
- Required in inspected Pini form: name, phone, email, consent.
- Optional message.
- Complex-level inquiry: optional multiple apartment sizes and minimum/maximum floor from the project's inquiry options.
- Apartment-level inquiry: selected projectId and unitId; no general size/floor preference controls.
- Pini validates fields, blocks repeated in-flight submission, retains form errors, and records locale/context.
- Hebrew must be added; existing Pini locale mapping only handles ka/en/ru.
BR-008 Save the inquiry first and offer optional phone OTP afterward. Skipping or failing OTP must not discard the inquiry or prevent ordinary routing. OTP is an added Aura step, not a claim about existing Pini functionality. Provider/channel, retry limits, duplicate handling, and verification lifecycle are unresolved.

## Admin users and access
BR-009 SuperAdmin is the owner, who has full access to all users, teams, leads, content, and settings. Only the owner holds this role.
BR-010 Team leads see their own teams, team work, and team leads/inquiries; they can assign a lead to a concrete agent in their team. Meaning of team-work visibility needs detail.
BR-011 Agents see only their own assigned leads. No access to other agents' or teams' leads.
BR-012 Editors manage website content, upload/edit complexes, and blogs. Editing authority is grantable to individual users, not globally to a role: one team lead may edit while another cannot. Exact permission granularity and publishing authority remain open. Content access must not expand lead visibility.
BR-013 Admin includes an active-lead Kanban and lead comments. Confirmed active columns: New, Not answered, Contacted, Viewing scheduled, and Negotiation. Remaining stages, transitions, comment edit/delete rights, and activity history are unresolved.

## Automatic distribution
BR-014 SuperAdmin sees all leads. New inquiries are automatically routed to teams/team leads; team leads distribute them to agents.
BR-015 One eligible team receives all new inquiries. With several teams, choose based on approximately equal numbers of not-yet-contacted leads, rather than historical total leads. No more advanced balancing in MVP.
BR-016 Routing workload is the number of leads in New plus Not answered in each team (BR-033). Routing eligibility, no-team fallback, reassignment, and duplicate treatment remain open. Equal-count ties rotate between the tied teams (BR-038). Do not silently implement proposals as confirmed rules.

## Deployment direction
BR-017 Use Cloudflare in the deployment architecture. Exact hosting, database, media storage, environments, domain, and OTP integrations will be planned after business discovery.

## Lead lifecycle and follow-up — owner discussion update
BR-018 Active leads appear on one Kanban board. Lost leads are outside the active board in a separate table; preserve their comments. Won leads are outside the active board in a separate table for customers who bought something. Final win/loss criteria and reopening rules remain open.
BR-019 Maximum two unanswered call attempts total, including the initial call from New. First unanswered attempt moves to Not answered; second unanswered attempt leads to Lost, subject to the mandatory explanation in BR-032. No additional two attempts after entering Not answered. Exact UI/automation sequence remains open; the system must not close a lead without the required explanation.
BR-020 System-generated follow-up notifications are required after call activity where follow-up is needed. Agents can also add a comment and set a reminder at an exact time (e.g. customer will be in town in two hours). Trigger recording, default timing, notification channel, snooze/completion, timezone, and overdue behavior remain open. MVP has no calling-provider integration, so calls must be recorded explicitly; a comment alone must not be assumed to prove a call occurred.
BR-021 Calling-provider integration (Zoho or another provider), AI call listening/transcription, and automatically generated call comments are future scope, excluded from MVP.

BR-022 Use one Contacted column in MVP for customers who answered, whether the agent is understanding their needs or discussing a specific apartment. Agents record those details in comments. Do not split this into needs-assessment/qualified/interested columns now. Adjustments may be considered after the team starts using the system; no additional stage is implied by this decision.

BR-023 Admin user creation: SuperAdmin can create users in every permitted role and grant/revoke individual-user permissions. The sole-owner SuperAdmin restriction in BR-009 still applies; this does not authorize additional SuperAdmins.
BR-024 Team leads may create agent users only. Each created agent is automatically assigned to the creating team lead's team. Team leads cannot use user creation to assign agents to another team, create higher roles, or grant content/other elevated permissions.
BR-025 SuperAdmin can change an agent's team. Handling of existing assigned leads, comments, pending reminders, and historical visibility when an agent transfers remains unresolved; do not automatically move those records without an agreed rule.

BR-026 Viewing scheduled is a separate active Kanban column. Appointment fields, reminder timing, rescheduling, cancellation/no-show behavior, and the post-viewing stage are not yet agreed.
BR-027 Admin users can see notifications in the admin header. Follow-up/reminder notifications must respect lead visibility. Notification presentation, read/unread handling, retention, and any additional delivery channels remain open.

BR-028 Negotiation is a separate active Kanban column for customers discussing a specific apartment price or purchase terms. This confirms the column, not mandatory stage order, stage-skipping rules, or the exact Won criterion; those remain open.

BR-029 A lead qualifies as Won after a purchase contract is signed. For MVP, the agent moves their assigned lead to Won manually; no automatic contract detection, e-signing, or payment verification is implied. Required closure details/evidence remain open.
BR-030 Won lead visibility follows ordinary lead scope: SuperAdmin sees everything; team leads see their team's Won leads; agents see their own Won leads. A content-editing permission grants no additional lead access.
BR-031 Lost leads use the same permissions and ownership scope as Active and Won leads: SuperAdmin accesses all; team leads access their team records; agents access their own assigned records. Do not introduce a Lost-only read-only restriction or additional visibility. Reopening transition rules remain open because Active/Won transition authority is not fully defined.

BR-032 Every move to Lost requires a non-empty explanatory comment. Lost includes both unanswered leads and other losses; agents can move their own leads to Lost for other reasons when explaining why. Preserve the explanation with the lead. Do not require two failed calls before a loss for another reason. Exact structured reason options remain open; unanswered is confirmed as a distinct reason.

BR-033 For automatic team distribution, count each team's leads in New plus Not answered, including both agent-assigned and agent-unassigned leads within those stages. Contacted, Viewing scheduled, Negotiation, Lost, and Won do not count. Count team backlog, not historical totals or per-agent totals; agents remain assigned by their team lead.

BR-034 After the first unanswered call, the automatic follow-up notification fires exactly 90 minutes later, including outside working hours. Do not postpone it to the next morning. The owner has now explicitly confirmed this timing; it is no longer merely a selected default. Manual comment-associated reminders retain their separately selected exact time. Whether agents can override/cancel the automatic reminder remains open.

BR-035 Due reminders appear as unread notifications in the agent's admin header. Delivery must not depend on the agent being online at the due time; unread notifications remain available when they return. Exact read/acknowledgement interaction, notification completion, and cancellation after lead state/assignment changes remain open.

BR-036 When a Lost customer returns, the assigned agent can manually reopen their own Lost lead into Contacted. Preserve the existing team/agent assignment and lead history, including prior comments and loss explanation. Do not create a duplicate lead or automatically redistribute it. Contacted is excluded from the New + Not answered routing workload.

BR-037 Moving a lead to Contacted (called answered in the owner's latest message) cancels its pending automatic 90-minute unanswered-call reminder. This is not a new Kanban column or a rename. Separately scheduled manual reminders remain intact. Behavior for an automatic notification already delivered, and cancellation on Lost/Won transitions, remain open.

BR-038 When eligible teams tie for the smallest New + Not answered count, rotate assignment between those tied teams. Prioritize the smallest backlog first; rotation breaks equal-count ties, not all assignments regardless of workload. Persist tie-break state and evaluate assignment atomically so concurrent inquiries do not undermine balancing. Eligibility and no-team fallback remain open.

## Team structure, leave and commissions — MVP scope expansion
BR-039 Each team has exactly one permanent team lead; each agent belongs to exactly one team. Teams remain separate under existing access rules. Temporary cover in BR-041 is a time-bounded delegation, not a second permanent lead.
BR-040 MVP includes vacation scheduling/requests, approval and annual leave-balance tracking. Owner requested 24 days/year; Georgian Labour Code Article 31 establishes at least 24 working days annually, not calendar days. Work schedules, holidays, leave-year definition, eligibility, carryover, accrual, cancellation and who approves non-agent leave remain open. Do not claim a simple counter is a complete payroll/legal-compliance system.
BR-041 Agent vacation requests require team-lead approval. A team lead going on vacation can select an agent to act temporarily as that team's lead. Exact delegation dates, same-team restriction, approval of the team lead's own vacation, temporary powers and restoration remain open; no automatic commission-rate change is implied.
BR-042 MVP includes commission calculation/tracking using apartment sale price, Aura agency earnings, and agent/team-lead percentages. Only SuperAdmin can set/change commission percentages. Defaults requested: agents 1%; team leads 0.5% from team earnings. Agent calculation base is confirmed as apartment sale price (BR-044). Team-lead calculation base is also apartment sale price (BR-045), not gross or net Aura earnings. Per-user versus global default settings, earning recognition, refunds, multiple agents, historic rate snapshots, currency/rounding and paid/unpaid tracking remain open. No payroll transfer or tax calculation is authorized by this feature.
BR-043 Automatic percentage progression based on monthly sales (example: two sales -> 1.1%) is post-MVP. The numeric example is not a finalized future rule; MVP uses SuperAdmin-controlled rates only.

BR-044 Agent commission is the agent rate multiplied by the apartment sale price, not Aura agency earnings. Default 1%: apartment sold for $100,000 -> agent commission $1,000. Only SuperAdmin changes rates. Contract sale-price entry/confirmation, earning recognition, historical snapshots, refunds and payment tracking remain open.

BR-045 Team-lead commission is the team-lead rate multiplied by apartment sale price for sales by their team. Default 0.5%: $100,000 sale -> $500 team-lead commission, separate from the agent's default $1,000. Both amounts use the same sale-price base, not one another's earnings or Aura revenue. Which team lead/rate applies during a transfer or temporary delegation remains unresolved; do not automatically grant acting agents extra commission.

BR-046 When a sale is recorded as Won, preserve the commission rates applied at closing and calculated commission amounts against its sale-price basis. Later user-rate changes apply to future sales and do not recalculate historical commissions. Historical deal corrections/refunds and the treatment of team transfers or temporary acting leads still need agreement; automatic changes to closed records are not permitted by this rule.

BR-047 During temporary team-lead cover, split the team-lead commission equally between the permanent lead and acting agent. With the default 0.5% rate, each receives 0.25% of apartment sale price: $100,000 sale -> $250 permanent lead + $250 acting agent. This divides the existing team-lead commission, not adds an additional 0.5%. An acting agent's ordinary agent commission on their own sale is separate. The owner said "0.25-0.25 something like this"; the equal-split interpretation was disclosed by the orchestrator. Acting share is half of the configured default team-lead rate, not half of the permanent lead's personal rate (BR-049). Permanent-lead remainder equals their personal team-lead rate minus acting share (BR-050). Eligibility is determined by contract signing date within the temporary-cover period (BR-048); exact date/time boundaries remain open. Preserve the applicable split in closed-sale snapshots once eligibility is agreed.

BR-048 Apply the temporary-cover team-lead commission split when the purchase contract signing date falls within the acting assignment period. Use the signing date, not the date the agent moves the lead to Won. Store the signing date separately from Won entry time; delayed entry must retain the correct temporary-cover recipients. Period boundary/timezone and rate-effective-date semantics remain open.

BR-049 Automatically set the acting team-lead share to half of the default team-lead rate: default 0.5% -> acting share 0.25% of apartment sale price. Do not base this automatic share on a permanent lead's customized personal rate. The acting share can be adjusted; under the existing commission authority rule, only SuperAdmin changes commission rates. Whether this is an override per cover assignment or user, how default changes apply to existing cover periods, and validation of overrides remain open. Permanent share is defined in BR-050. BR-047's 0.25%/0.25% remains correct at default rates; do not generalize to 50/50 of personalized rates.

BR-050 During temporary cover, the permanent lead receives their personal team-lead commission rate minus the acting share. Default automatic acting share is half the configured default team-lead rate, subject to SuperAdmin adjustment. Example: permanent personal rate 0.8%, default lead rate 0.5% -> acting 0.25%, permanent 0.55%. The original personal-rate pool is divided, not increased. Agent sale commission remains separate. Validation for an acting share greater than the personal-rate pool, override scope and future default-rate changes remain open.

BR-051 For MVP, SuperAdmin approves permanent team leads' vacation requests, including the nominated temporary replacement. Team leads continue approving their agents' vacation requests. Additional approval/management roles are planned after MVP and must not be added to current role scope. Delegation activation follows an approved request/cover period; exact date boundaries and temporary powers remain open. Approval for editors/non-team staff remains open.

BR-052 During approved temporary cover, the acting agent gains team-management powers for their team: view team active leads, assign leads to team agents, create agents automatically in that team, and approve other agents' vacation requests. Delegated powers end automatically when the approved cover period finishes. Acting lead cannot approve their own vacation or change commission rates. Content permissions remain individual and are not inherited through temporary cover.
BR-053 Temporary cover does not grant team-wide access to Lost or Won leads. Acting agent retains ordinary agent access to their own Lost and Won leads; they cannot use delegation to access other agents' closed records. Permanent team leads and SuperAdmin retain their existing scopes. This is a temporary-delegation exception, not a change to ordinary Active/Lost/Won role permissions. Notification and API access must respect the same scopes.
